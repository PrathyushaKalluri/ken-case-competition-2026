# Survey Guide QA — Google Form, redesigned to 17 questions after field feedback

Companion to the team's existing `survey_guide.md` (the compact Google-Forms-ready adaptation of the original S0-S30 instrument). What's here is a distinct, complementary pass built for **generic, self-administered WhatsApp distribution to any Indian household** — not tied to any specific city or named community.

**Revision history**: the first pass (33 questions) was built for Hyderabad/Visakhapatnam gated communities specifically and pressure-tested with two simulated cognitive pilots. Direct field feedback said it was too long to realistically get completed and too narrowly scoped to two cities. It was redesigned down to **17 questions**, generic to any city, with every remaining question explicitly mapped to a rail (Voice / Payments & Authorisation / Logistics) or one of the competition's own 10 answers, and every place the original asked a chain of related sub-questions collapsed into a single grid question. `Survey_Plain_Language_v2.md` now holds this redesigned version (filename kept stable rather than forked into a new file); the two pilot transcripts below are kept as-is since their underlying lessons — no unexplained jargon, don't assume breakdown drama, don't bundle two decisions into one question — carried forward into the shorter version.

**Final deliverable**: `output/docx/Ken_2026_Household_Survey_Google_Form_Guide.docx`.

## Files in this folder

- `Survey_Plain_Language_v2.md` — the current, redesigned 17-question Google Form script (source markdown for the Word doc).
- `WhatsApp_Message.md` — the message to post in resident WhatsApp groups (generic, names no city), plus a shorter reminder variant and posting notes.
- `Pilot_1_Hyderabad_homemaker.md`, `Pilot_2_Vizag_homemaker.md` — full cognitive-pretest transcripts and critiques from the two AI-simulated respondents that tested the original 33-question draft, kept as process evidence (the competition's own Q2 criteria explicitly credit "chat logs, including logs of you working the problem through with an AI"). Their findings are summarized in the current Word doc's appendix.

## Why this exists alongside `survey_guide.md`

`survey_guide.md` is a faithful, comprehensive Google-Forms translation of the original 30-item S0-S30 instrument, built for the team's original four channels (My Home, standalone, Eluru, IIITH). This guide is a much shorter (17-question), city-agnostic variant optimized for actual completion rates on a WhatsApp-forwarded link, with every question tied explicitly to a rail or a competition answer. Both draw on the same underlying research design and don't contradict each other — use whichever fits the distribution channel and how much depth you need per response.
