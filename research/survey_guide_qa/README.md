# Survey Guide QA — Google Form, tested against 2 cognitive pilots

Companion to the team's existing `survey_guide.md` (the compact Google-Forms-ready adaptation of the original S0-S30 instrument). What's here is a distinct, complementary pass built specifically for **self-administered distribution into Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam gated-community WhatsApp groups**, mostly to housewives, with a documented WhatsApp circulation message, an explicit false-positive screening strategy, and two simulated cognitive-pilot tests run before finalizing (the same pretesting method — "what did this mean to you, how did you work out your answer, was an option missing" — the team's own plan already prescribes for its in-person pilots).

**Final deliverable**: `output/docx/Ken_2026_Household_Survey_Google_Form_Guide.docx`.

## Files in this folder

- `Survey_Plain_Language_v2.md` — the tested, final version of the Google Form script (source markdown for the Word doc).
- `WhatsApp_Message.md` — the exact message to post in community WhatsApp groups, plus a shorter reminder variant and posting notes.
- `Pilot_1_Hyderabad_homemaker.md`, `Pilot_2_Vizag_homemaker.md` — full cognitive-pretest transcripts and critiques from two independent AI-simulated respondents, kept as process evidence (the competition's own Q2 criteria explicitly credit "chat logs, including logs of you working the problem through with an AI").

## Why this exists alongside `survey_guide.md`

`survey_guide.md` is a faithful, comprehensive Google-Forms translation of the original 30-item S0-S30 instrument, built for the team's original four channels (My Home, standalone, Eluru, IIITH). This guide is a shorter, simpler-language variant purpose-built for two specific cities (Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam) and an audience skewed toward housewives with varying English fluency, plus the WhatsApp-specific trust and false-positive concerns that come with open group distribution. Both draw on the same underlying research design and don't contradict each other — use whichever fits the distribution channel.
