**Team statement — LOCKED 2026-09-10, exactly 50 words**

> Most teams will have one city and one side of this. We have places from all three tiers and three languages, and through family a carpenter running a ten-man crew who subcontracts the plumbers, electricians and technicians. We are the customer, and we know the people who do the work.

**What each part is doing:**
- *"one city and one side"* — names both gaps other teams have, in the opening sentence, so everything after reads as proof rather than description.
- *"all three tiers and three languages"* — tier structure rather than place names: Hyderabad (T1), Vizag (T2), Eluru and Hajipur, Bihar (T3). Says these are structurally different markets, not just different addresses. Two states, north and south.
- *"a ten-man crew who subcontracts the plumbers, electricians and technicians"* — the hard, unclaimable fact. One relationship is a door to a whole trade network, not to a single person.
- *"We are the customer, and we know the people who do the work"* — problem-market fit and go-to-market in one plain sentence: demand and supply, both already held. Deliberately jargon-free.

**Word-count warning:** this is exactly at the 50 cap. If the live form counts differently (hyphens and "ten-man" are the usual culprits), drop "the" from *"the plumbers"* for one word of slack.
