KEY VERIFIED FIGURES (safe to cite directly, with this exact source):
- Monthly complaint registrations (average): grew from 37,062 in 2017 to 1,70,585 in 2025.
- Call volumes: grew from 12,553 in December 2015 to 1,55,138 in December 2024 (more than tenfold).
- July 2025: NCH facilitated refunds totalling Rs. 2.72 crore by resolving 7,256 consumer grievances across 27 sectors (up from 1,079 grievances / Rs. 62 lakh in April 2025).
- E-commerce was the LARGEST category for refund-related complaints in July 2025: 3,594 cases, Rs. 1.34 crore in refunds. Travel and Tourism followed (Rs. 31 lakh). Household appliances were not named among the top categories in this specific release.
- Digital channel adoption: ~65% of grievances now registered via online/digital channels; WhatsApp-based grievance registration grew from 3% (March 2023) to 20% (March 2025).
- Convergence partners (companies that opted in to receive real-time complaint data and respond within 30 days): grew from 263 (2017) to 1,142 (Sept 2025).
- GST Reforms 2025 tracking: 3,981 GST-related calls logged as of Oct 2, 2025 — 31% queries, 69% formal grievances. Consumer durables (including TVs, ACs, monitors, dishwashers) were named as a sector affected by GST rate changes (28%→18%), relevant context for pricing-related complaints, not repair-coordination complaints specifically.

RELEVANCE: Verifies the *scale and growth* of India's institutional consumer-grievance infrastructure and culture — useful context for arguing that Indian consumers are increasingly willing to formally escalate service failures (relevant to Q9 market-readiness reasoning), and for the general "delegation to institutions is already normalized in India" thesis (context.md Part 5, "The Great Rewiring" column).
