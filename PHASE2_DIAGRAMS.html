Kokila: any brand, any machine, one trip.

For India's tier-2 and tier-3 repair agencies: any brand, any machine, fixed on the first visit within the customer's approved budget, or the second trip is on us.

Kokila is a white-label AI agent that works inside a local service agency's own phone number, WhatsApp and ticket book. Before a technician leaves the shop, she finds out what's wrong inside the machine, secures the part and gets the customer's budget approved. The first visit becomes a repair, not a discovery.

The master agent and its sub-agents

Q7

The name

Kokila.Kokilaben asked "Rasode mein kaun tha?" (who was in the kitchen?). Our Kokila asks "Machine mein kya tha?" (what's wrong inside the machine?) and won't let anyone leave the shop until she knows. Tagline for agencies: "Pehli trip mein fix. Baaki Kokila dekh legi." (Fixed on the first trip. Kokila handles the rest.)

Q8

Who else could build this

Two questions separate the field: who pays the company, and does it work across many brands? Kokila is the only one that pays off for independent, multi-brand agencies.

Best chance: Servify. It already runs after-sales for brands like Apple and Samsung, coordinating about 18,000 service centres. It hasn't built Kokila because its customers are the brands, and brands have no reason to help independent multi-brand agencies or share fault data with them. That's where most tier-2 and tier-3 repairs happen.
